[ ARTIST STATEMENT ]

BEYOND
THE FRAME.

AN ONGOING EXPLORATION OF SOUND, MATTER, RHYTHM AND IMAGE.

A sonic identity within the Granular Spectrum artist network.

Shakti is a Colombian electronic music project active since 2014, moving between experimental techno, deep structures, hypnotic rhythms and audiovisual expression.

Alongside music production, the project develops visual and sonic environments, tools and live experiences. Shakti is also the founder and curator of Granular Spectrum, an independent platform for experimental electronic music.
