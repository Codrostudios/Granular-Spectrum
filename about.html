Granular Spectrum exists as a space for sound that resists easy classification. A platform for electronic music shaped by experimentation, tension, texture and the constant search for new sonic territories.

Rather than following a fixed genre or formula, the label approaches each release as an independent statement — a document of an idea, a process or a particular state of sound.
