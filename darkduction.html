DARKDUCTION
ALIAS / DRKDCTN




ACTIVE SINCE 2014

Darkduction emerged in 2014 as a multifaceted electronic music project rooted in experimental, minimalist and hypnotic techno. Its sound moves through unusual textures, reduced structures and shifting rhythms, testing the limits between the physical energy of the dancefloor and abstract sonic exploration.

Beyond music production and composition, the project extends into audiovisual works and the development of tools for music production. This parallel practice informs a process where sound, image and technology become connected parts of the same language.

As founder of Granular Spectrum, Darkduction also develops a platform for experimental electronic music, advanced musical projects and audiovisual research.
