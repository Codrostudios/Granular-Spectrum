[ ARTIST STATEMENT ]

BEYOND
THE FRAME.

AN ONGOING EXPLORATION OF SOUND, MATTER, RHYTHM AND IMAGE.

Sound as a physical territory. Rhythm as a shifting structure. Noise as a form of memory.

Darkduction is a Colombian electronic music project active since 2014, moving between experimental techno, deep structures, hypnotic rhythms and audiovisual expression.

Alongside music production, the project develops visual and sonic environments, tools and live experiences. Darkduction is also the founder and curator of Granular Spectrum, an independent platform for experimental electronic music.

Its work explores the unstable border between structure and collapse, club functionality and abstraction, physical pulse and immersive sound.
